Architecture Overview

Sentinel Shield – System Architecture

Sentinel Shield was conceived to address the critical business threat of financial fraud in high-volume transaction environments.

  • Data Preprocessing: Normalized transaction amounts, frequency metrics, and categorical account behaviors.
  • Class Imbalance Handling: Addressed highly skewed transaction datasets typical of real-world banking fraud.
  • Model Evaluation: Evaluated using Precision, Recall, and AUC-ROC curves to minimize false positives while detecting suspicious outliers.
  • Business Value: Translates raw anomaly scores into risk flags to protect institutional and customer assets.
